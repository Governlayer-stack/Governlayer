Solution

Runtime Governance,
Replayable Evidence.

GovernLayer sits in the decision path of every AI agent in your organization — monitoring, validating, and recording every action to a regulator-replayable ledger.

Continuous Drift Detection

Behavioral baselines plus embedding-distance scoring. Real-time alerts when an agent's behavior moves outside its safety manifold.

Achonye Multi-LLM Engine

Cost-optimized routing across local and cloud models. Trivial decisions stay local for efficiency; critical decisions trigger multi-model consensus with voting and chain-of-verification for reliability.

Cryptographic Audit Ledger

SHA-256 hash-chained from genesis. Every decision tied to inputs, policy, and model output. Regulator-replayable — reconstruct any decision exactly as it happened.

Not point-in-time audit (Vanta). Not just guardrails (Lakera). Runtime governance with replayable evidence.
