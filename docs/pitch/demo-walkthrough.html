Step 1 alone is fine — reading records is a normal operation. Step 2 alone could be legitimate. But the full chain — read, stage, redistribute — matches a financial exfiltration pattern. Multi-step causal validation is part of our AGI safety research roadmap; provisional patent filed (GOVERNLAYER-001), non-provisional conversion planned Q4 2026.
